# Rules of the Method

*   **Who asks writes the WHAT and the WHY, who executes decides the HOW.**
    *   Defining the boundaries gives the executor the freedom to solve the problem efficiently.

*   **Assumptions are written to be knocked down.**
    *   Explicitly stating what you believe to be true allows the executor to correct fundamental errors early.

*   **Never hand your own findings to the one who will give an opinion.**
    *   If you give them your conclusions, you will just get your own frame back with a stamp of approval.

*   **Short and human for discovery, long and written for what will be built.**
    *   Exploration requires agility; engineering requires a permanent, unambiguous record.

*   **An acceptance criterion the executor cannot round off.**
    *   Failure must return a non-zero code or a specifically named gap, not a silent omission.

*   **"Inconclusive" beats a confident guess.**
    *   Knowing what you don't know is actionable; acting on hallucinated facts is destructive.

*   **Every claim carries source and date or NOT VERIFIED.**
    *   Trust requires a trail. If it can't be traced, it must be flagged.

*   **The reviewer writes rules for the future, not verdicts about the past.**
    *   Reviews exist to improve the next output, not just grade the current one.
