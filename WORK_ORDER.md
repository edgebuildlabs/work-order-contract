# Work Order

## Context
[What is not in the files: current phase, transient context, what has already been proven.]

## Assumptions
[What this request assumes to be true; write these so the executor can knock them down if wrong.]

## Goal
[The ultimate objective and the specific question to be answered.]

## Acceptance Criteria
[What "done" means; verifiable conditions the response must meet to be usable.]

## Already Decided
[Decisions that are locked in; do not reopen these without new, compelling evidence.]

## Out of Scope
[What should be explicitly ignored or not attempted during this task.]

## Read in This Order
[Named files to read, in sequence, and the stopping criterion to avoid reading everything.]

## Evidence Rules
[Separate fact from inference and opinion. Every claim needs a source and date, or NOT VERIFIED. "Inconclusive" is a valid answer.]

## Deliverable
[The shape, format, length, and destination of the output.]

## Do Not
[Explicit constraints on actions, tools, or behaviors to avoid.]
